Privacy Policy

Last updated: 19 September 2026.

Jack Pfefer Archive Utility is a personal, non-commercial archival workflow operated solely by its owner.

Google user data

The utility uses Google OAuth solely to allow the owner to authenticate rclone with the owner's own Google Drive account.

Google Drive access is used to list files and folders, upload archival material, and verify that the owner's local archive matches the copy stored in Google Drive.

Storage

OAuth credentials and access tokens used by rclone are stored locally on the owner's computer. They are not published on this website.

Sharing

Google user data obtained through the OAuth connection is not sold, rented, or provided to advertisers or unrelated third parties.

Purpose

Google Drive access is used only for maintaining the owner's personal wrestling research archive.
